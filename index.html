What we do

One shop for the whole vehicle

Routine maintenance to the repairs nobody else wants to touch — cars, trucks, Jeeps, and motor homes. Here's what rolls through the bays.

Maintenance & Oil

Oil and filter changes, fluids, belts, brakes, and factory-schedule service to keep small problems from becoming big ones.

Exhaust

Mufflers, pipes, and exhaust repair — quieter rides, better flow, and a look under the car before it turns into a bigger job.

A/C Service

Florida-summer air conditioning that actually blows cold — diagnosis, recharge, and repair so the cabin stays comfortable.

Diagnostics

Check-engine light on? We read the codes and, more importantly, find the real cause — then explain it in plain English.

General Repair

Suspension, steering, cooling, electrical, and the odd jobs in between — the wide-ranging fixes a trusted garage takes on.

RVs & Motor Homes

We've worked on everything from motor homes to Jeeps. Bring the big rig — we've got the room and the know-how for it.
